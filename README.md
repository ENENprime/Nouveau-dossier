# A Student's website ENGLISH Version
A website made for students to test their capacities when faced with external and different papers.

## Features 
* **Calculator** A user friendly calculator used for simple **ARITHMETIC Computation**
* **A timer** To time your self
* **Past papers** A collection of randomly selected papers put at the disposal of students to test their capacities.
* **A page used for Physolophical thoughs** I designed a spacial page for us to really understand what the world of tomorrow is waiting from us.
* **Relaxing and Reconcentration** Here you will find a collection of well selected songs to accompany you in your mission.
* **Intergrated FR version** It was made both in english and french for easy accessibility. 


## Build with
* **HTML** The skeleton of the project
* **CSS** The outer view and beauty of the site
* **JavaScript** Mainly used for the calculator

## How to use it
This site was made use friendly and so do not require specific skills for usage .
Just click on the link above to view the site.
